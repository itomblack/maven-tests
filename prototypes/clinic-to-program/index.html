A quick hormone check-in

Weight and hormones are closely linked — especially for women over 40. Three questions to make sure nothing's being missed.

Start check-in

Have you noticed night sweats or hot flashes?

Has your cycle become irregular in the last year?

Worth a closer look

These symptoms can intersect with weight management.

Hormonal shifts can make weight harder to manage and affect sleep and energy — which in turn affect your GLP-1 progress. Treating both together often works better than either alone.

Your GLP-1 care team can coordinate with Hormone Care so it's one connected plan.

Why weight loss feels impossible for women

You've tried Noom. Weight Watchers. Keto. Intermittent fasting. You'd lose 15 lbs, then gain back 20. Because every program ignores three things:

  1. 01

    Your hormones are working against you

    Perimenopause, postpartum, PCOS — they all slow your metabolism and increase hunger. Generic programs don't account for this.

  2. 02

    Willpower doesn't work long-term

    Hunger hormones (ghrelin and leptin) fight back when you restrict calories. You're not weak — you're fighting biology.

  3. 03

    Your body changes as you lose weight

    What worked in month 1 stops working in month 3. You plateau and nobody adjusts your approach.
